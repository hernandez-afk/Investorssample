About Us

A global cross-platform licensor and interactive entertainment Group — an iconic gaming industry brand that transcends generations and audiences.

Atari is an interactive entertainment company and an iconic gaming industry brand that transcends generations and audiences. The company is globally recognized for its multi-platform, interactive entertainment, and licensed products.

Atari owns and/or manages a portfolio of more than 400 unique games and franchises, including world-renowned brands like Asteroids®, Centipede®, Missile Command®, Pong®, and RollerCoaster Tycoon®. The Atari family of brands includes game developers Digital Eclipse and Nightdive Studios, the publishing label Infogrames, and the community-based sites AtariAge and MobyGames.

Atari has offices in New York and Paris. Visit us online at www.Atari.com.

Atari is listed on the Euronext Growth Paris stock exchange and affiliated with Nasdaq International under the ticker PONGF on the U.S. OTC market. This investor site provides information dedicated to shareholders, investors, and financial market professionals.
