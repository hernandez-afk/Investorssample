Our mission

Atari is an interactive entertainment company and an iconic gaming industry brand that transcends generations and audiences. The company is globally recognized for its multi-platform, interactive entertainment, and licensed products. Atari owns and/or manages a portfolio of more than 400 unique games and franchises, including world-renowned brands like Asteroids®, Centipede®, Missile Command®, Pong®, and RollerCoaster Tycoon®. The Atari family of brands includes game developers Digital Eclipse and Nightdive Studios, the publishing label Infogrames, and the community-based sites AtariAge and MobyGames. Atari has offices in New York and Paris. Visit us online at www.Atari.com.

Our Key Priorities

Video Games

Atari continues to expand its portfolio of PC, console, and mobile games — launching new titles alongside beloved classics like Pong®, Asteroids®, Centipede®, and Missile Command®.

Consumer Hardware

From the Atari 2600+ to the 7800+, Atari's consumer hardware brings back the feel of the originals with modern, HDMI-ready home consoles built for today's living rooms.

Licensing

Atari's licensing business partners with brands and creators to bring its games and characters to new products and experiences for the next generation of gamers.

Atari X

Launched in 2022, Atari X consolidates Atari's blockchain interests under one operation, building an ecosystem that connects community, gaming, and utility.
